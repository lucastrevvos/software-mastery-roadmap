DAY 01 · 2026-09-23

Questions, verb forms & natural phrasing

from today's chat
ORIGINAL

Who never did it.

NATURAL

Who hasn't done that?

Pattern: use the present perfect for life experience up to now: have/has + past participle.
ORIGINAL

I was to think in add a English teacher...

NATURAL

I was thinking about adding an English teacher...

Patterns: was/were + verb-ing for an ongoing thought; after about, use a gerund: about adding; use an before a vowel sound: an English teacher.
ORIGINAL

I did a lot mistakes in programming.

NATURAL

I've made a lot of mistakes in programming.

Collocation: English uses make a mistake, not do a mistake. Also remember a lot of + noun.
ORIGINAL

Do you working with what now Ethan?

NATURAL

What are you working with now, Ethan?

Question structure: with the present continuous, move be before the subject: What + are + you + working with?. Do not add do.
ORIGINAL IDEA

delete without where is classic

NATURAL PROGRAMMING ENGLISH

Deleting without a WHERE clause is a classic and unforgettable mistake.

Recall

  1. Complete: “What ___ you working on right now?”
  2. Complete: “I've ___ many mistakes while learning.”
  3. Choose: “thinking about add” or “thinking about adding”?
  4. Complete: “Who ___ done that before?”

Transfer

Write these naturally in English without translating word by word:

  1. Com que stack você está trabalhando agora?
  2. Eu já cometi muitos erros programando.
  3. Eu estava pensando em adicionar outra pessoa ao grupo.
  4. Quem nunca fez isso?
