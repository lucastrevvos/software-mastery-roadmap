DAY 03 · 2026-09-26

Retest: auxiliaries before new grammar

unresolved-pattern retest

There was not enough genuinely new English to justify inventing a new weakness. Today's lesson retests the highest-impact pattern that reappeared: auxiliaries must carry tense, leaving the main verb in its base form.

ORIGINAL

I dont knew that interview hat techniques questions...

NATURAL

I didn't know the interview would have technical questions, so I was calmer.

Core rule: didn't + base verb. The past tense is already carried by did, so say didn't know, never didn't knew.
ORIGINAL

I did an interview for senior engineer with .net and event-driven.

NATURAL

I had an interview for a Senior .NET Engineer role focused on event-driven architecture.

Professional collocation: in natural English you usually have an interview. For a job, role also makes the sentence clearer.
ORIGINAL

She just read the questions and recorded to send to team technical...

NATURAL

She just read the questions and recorded my answers to send to the technical team.

Word order: descriptive adjectives normally come before nouns: technical team, technical questions, senior role.

Recall

  1. Choose: “I didn't knew” or “I didn't know”?
  2. Complete: “Why ___ they ask you about CQRS?”
  3. Complete: “I ___ an interview yesterday.”
  4. Choose: “team technical” or “technical team”?

Repair

  1. I didn't expected technical questions.
  2. What did she asked you?
  3. I did an interview with the client.
  4. They sent my answers to the team technical.

Transfer

  1. Say naturally: “Eu não sabia que teria perguntas técnicas.”
  2. Say naturally: “Eu fiz uma entrevista para uma vaga sênior.”
  3. Answer: “What did they ask you about event-driven architecture?”
  4. Answer: “How did you feel during the interview?”

Retest gate

In the next spontaneous conversation, produce three correct past structures without looking:

  1. I didn't + base verb
  2. What did + subject + base verb?
  3. I had an interview...

If did + past form reappears, the auxiliary pattern remains ACTIVE.

DAY 02 · 2026-09-24

Build the verb correctly before adding vocabulary

from real conversation

Today's pattern: most errors came from choosing the right words but building the verb phrase with the wrong structure. The priority is to make the core sentence automatic.

ORIGINAL

Why you stated in JS?

NATURAL

Why did you start with JavaScript?

Past questions: question word + did + subject + base verb.
ORIGINAL

Where you from?

NATURAL

Where are you from?

Questions with BE: move am/is/are before the subject.
ORIGINAL

If you want I can to show you.

NATURAL

If you want, I can show you.

Modal verbs: use the base verb without to.
ORIGINAL

She like to help the people. / She's need to use...

NATURAL

She likes helping people. / She needs to use it...

Third person: he/she/it usually takes -s.
ORIGINAL

Know PHP help me a lot.

NATURAL

Knowing PHP helped me a lot.

Verb as subject: an action used as the subject often takes the -ing form.
ORIGINAL

I felt that I can't do nothing without Google.

NATURAL

I felt like I couldn't do anything without Google.

Tense + negatives: keep the past frame consistent and avoid double negatives.
ORIGINAL

I will to sleep now.

NATURAL

I'm going to sleep now.

Natural intention: with will: I will sleep, never I will to sleep.
